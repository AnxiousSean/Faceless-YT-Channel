# WATCHED (working title: CAM 1) — storyboard (not animated yet)

**Original fiction** written for the channel. It is not a real account, so the video is never titled or described as "TRUE".
Runtime **4:00 (7200 frames)**. Horror Ink look. Title word: **WATCHED**.

## Logline
Theo works nights from home. Whenever he steps away from his desk, his webcam light blinks on by itself.
The recordings show a figure in the dark hallway behind his chair, one step closer every night.

## Cast
- **Theo** (lead): warm brown skin `#8D5A3B`, short dark hair, grey hoodie `#4A4F5C`, dark jeans. Tired eyebrows; they do all the acting.
- **Jess** (Theo's older sister): light skin `#F1C7A3`, long auburn hair `#7A3B1E`, green sweater. Seen only at the end.
- **Officer**: medium skin `#C99A72`, navy cap, navy shirt. Seen only in the reveal, mostly as a flashlight beam.
- **The Watcher** (menace, `ShadowSelf`): pure black, taller than Theo, red outline, red slit eyes. **Only ever seen inside camera footage and for the 3 frames of the jump scare.** Never lit, never fully shown.

## Sets
1. **Theo's apartment**, side-on. Desk centre with laptop (the main light, cold blue-white glow on his face). Office chair. **Dark hallway opening on the left** behind the chair, with a coat rack and a closet door left ajar. Kitchen on the right with a fridge (warm light when opened). Window with streetlight glow.
2. **Laptop screen close-up**: a support-chat window, plus the `CAM 1` app (recording thumbnails, a timestamp, a red `● REC` / `● LIVE` badge).
3. **Webcam lens close-up**: black lens, tiny red indicator light.
4. **Phone close-up**: lock screen with pop-up texts.
5. **Night road** for the drive (parallax: far hills, near street lights, headlights).
6. **Apartment exterior + hallway closet** with police lights pulsing red and blue.
7. **Jess's living room**: couch, lamp, TV with a standby light.

## Story HUD
- Top-left clock: `11:48 PM` → `2:05 AM` → `NIGHT 2…5` → `3:12 AM` → `3:31 AM`. Pops on each jump.
- Pop-ups: `37 new recordings`, unknown texts, `Calling Jess…`, `CAM 2 connected`, `1 new recording – 3:12 AM`.
