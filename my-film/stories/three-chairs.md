# THREE CHAIRS — story outline (not animated yet)

Inspired by: Al Jazeera, "Trump, Xi and Putin to hold first three-way summit in China" (Nov 2026).
Told as a fable: three neighbours who have never once sat at the same table. No real leaders, flags or logos.
Runtime 2:30 (4500 frames). Pixel look. Pip's item after Day 1: a red bow tie.

Cast
- Pip (tangerine), the host. Pip is the one who sends the invitations.
- Bram (new, steel-blue, tall and square-headed), the loud one from the top floor.
- Lune (new, jade-green, small and round), the quiet one from across the courtyard.
- Each has their own Shadow (violet glow). The three Shadows grow when the talks go badly.
- The "rockets": each neighbour keeps a giant firework on their balcony, aimed at the other two.
